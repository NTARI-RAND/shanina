# Shanina — Open Questions

*The living open-questions deliverable required by Janus-Facing Architecture (standard check 7). Reviewed and re-shipped with each release; staleness is nonconformance. An entry leaves only by resolution or by an explicit values call made by the people who live with the outcome. Invariant IDs cite the registry in `JFA/jfa-conformance-suite.py`. This document is the canonical home of the concept doc's "Open questions."*

Shanina is an **education-layer knowledge commons** (Part VI): anchored, citable observational claims on a tiered evidence model — the same rails that make trade trustworthy making knowledge trustworthy.

## 1. The name — RESOLVED
**Problem.** The concept doc's working name was "JFA," which collided head-on with Janus-Facing Architecture — the standard the whole canon is held to.
**Constraints inherited.** None architectural; pure confusion cost.
**Status.** **Resolved 2026-07-22 by decision (Jodson): the project is named Shanina.** The concept doc is updated throughout and the working title is retired. Entry retained as record per the removal discipline.

## 2. "Verified Community Event" — corroboration or truth authority?
**Problem.** Clusters of independent captures "graduate" an event to Verified status. The education layer forbids a truth authority: the commons anchors and weighs claims, it never adjudicates fact — a "verified true" flag from any center is off-architecture.
**Constraints inherited.** Promotion thresholds as explicit structure (the doc already refuses moderator whim) with full provenance kept — that is corroboration, and it conforms; the *label* "Verified" is the risk, because it reads as certification. Consider "Corroborated Community Event," and keep every promotion contestable.
**Status.** Open — mechanics are close to conformant; naming and contest path to settle.

## 3. Sybil precedes citation-weight (Part VI invariant)
**Problem.** Instrument calibration scores and corroboration counts will size observation weight — and eventually visibility, event promotion, and any contributor recognition. Fabricated instruments or coordinated false captures could manufacture "independent" corroboration.
**Constraints inherited.** Sybil-resistance must be settled before citation count or reputation sizes any real reward — the same gate as before credit turns on (COV-6). Fake-instrument collusion is the too-clean-signature analog: detectable as structure, flagged for contest, never auto-adjudicated. Calibration-against-Tier-0 is already an earned, purchase-free qualifier — a good bone.
**Status.** Open — required before Phase 3 campaigns hand out anything that functions as reward.

## 4. Database license (the concept doc's remaining licensing call)
**Problem.** ODbL 1.0 (share-alike) vs. CC0/CC BY (maximal scientific reuse) for the observation database — sharpened by the research value of sensor streams.
**Constraints inherited.** The commons stays open regardless (stated, keep it); citation legible and forkable — whatever license, a claim, its references, and their weight must ship re-derivable (Part VI). Code AGPL-3.0 and docs CC BY-SA are already settled (GOV-10 satisfied there).
**Status.** Open — a genuine values call between share-alike ethos and research uptake.

## 5. Passive sensor streams (design spike before Phase 3)
**Problem.** Battery cost, consent UX, anonymization architecture, and data volume for background magnetometer/GNSS contribution are all unquantified.
**Constraints inherited.** Strictly opt-in, plain-language consent, on-device anonymization, regional aggregation before any surface, published battery budget — all already stated; treat them as invariants of the feature, not aspirations. Privacy-first is entrenched at the top of the canon (bylaws §1.4).
**Status.** Open — spike before Phase 3 commits.

## 6. Remaining concept-doc questions
**Problem.** Timestamp-discipline spec for occultation-grade contributions; school partnership strategy; relations with existing citizen networks (occultation, meteor, variable-star, space-weather groups) as allies.
**Constraints inherited.** Claims anchored as claims with full provenance (Part VI); the calibration ladder doubles as pedagogy — the school program is the education layer being literal.
**Status.** Open.

## 7. Monetization boundary
**Problem.** Subscription on advanced chart features is the stated model; the boundary needs to be mechanical, not aspirational.
**Constraints inherited.** Reputation is earned, never sold — no paid placement anywhere in interpretation ranking, event promotion, or observation weight (Part VI); the commons and the dataset stay open regardless of payment status (stated — keep it entrenched).
**Status.** Committed as a constraint; mechanics open.
