Shanina — nimi en pali ona
==========================

toki lawa (Toki Pona)
lipu ni li lipu lili pi toki pona. NTARI li pali e ona kepeken nasin lawa
"P2-002". lipu suli li lipu "Concept.txt" lon toki Inli (tenpo suno
2026-07-29); ona taso li lipu lon. ilo sona li pali e lipu ni, la kulupu li
kama lukin e ona kepeken nasin lawa "P2-002" kipisi 3.1.

Note (English)
This is a condensed community rendering under NTARI policy P2-002. The
complete document is the English original Concept.txt (snapshot 2026-07-29).
Machine-assisted draft pending community review per P2-002 section 3.1.

sina lukin e pakala lon toki ni la o pona e ona: o pali e "fork" lon
https://github.com/NTARI-RAND/shanina, o pana e "pull request". pana sina li
pona tawa mi mute.

---


ni li seme
----------

Shanina li ilo tu lon insa pi sewi wan.

- ilo nanpa wan li ilo pi nasin mun (astrology). ona li toki tan ma sina, tan
  mun pi ken lukin lon sewi sina lon tenpo pimeja ni.
- ilo nanpa tu li poki sona pi mun. ni li open tawa jan ale. ona li lon sewi
  pi sona "JPL". jan mute li pana e sona tan ilo toki (phone). jan pi ilo
  lukin mun (telescope) en tomo sona li pana e sona pona mute.

ilo tu ni li wile e ilo ante: nasin mun li pana e wile tawa jan mute tawa
lukin sewi, la poki sona li kama tan lukin ni. wawa pi kulupu li ni: ilo
lukin suli wan li lukin e sewi wan tan ma wan taso, taso ilo lili mute mute
li lukin e sewi ale lon ma ale lon tenpo ale. nimi wawa: mute li wawa tawa
suli.


nasin pali pi tenpo pimeja
--------------------------

lukin -> sitelen -> sona -> wan

ilo li toki e mun pi ken lukin, kin la ona li toki e ni: mun ni li poka e
lipu mun sina (natal chart). jan li sitelen e mun kepeken ilo. ilo li pana e
sona mun tan lipu pi kulupu. sona li tawa poki sona pi kulupu, kin la mun ni
li awen lon lipu tenpo pi jan ni lon tenpo ale.


kulupu tu wan pi sona
---------------------

sona pi kulupu li pana e sona sin lon poka pi sona "JPL"; ona li weka ala e
ona. kulupu pi sona li lon nasin sona, li lon ala ilo: ilo toki wan li ken
pona lon nasin wan, li ken ike lon nasin ante. ilo ale li jo e nimi ona lon
poki, en nanpa pi pona ona. ilo li toki sama Tier 0 lon mun pi sona la ona li
kama jo e wawa. nasin suli li ni: sewi pi sona li pona e ilo, kin la ilo pona
li sona e ijo pi sona ala. taso ilo toki li ken ala sona e suli, e tawa, e
sike pi ma (Earth): ilo suli ante li sona e ni kepeken nanpa pona mute.

- Tier 0 — sona sewi: sona pi "JPL" en sona pi ma ("GNSS"). sona ale ante li
  kama sona tan ni.
- Tier 1 — sona tan ilo suli: ilo lukin mun, ilo sitelen suli, ilo pi tomo
  sona. sona ni li ken kama sona awen. kin la kulupu pi ilo toki mute li Tier
  1: ilo "magnetometer" en ilo "GNSS" en ilo tenpo pona pi ilo toki mute mute
  li pali sama ilo suli wan. jan sona li pali e ni lon tenpo pini: ilo
  "CrowdMag" pi "NOAA", en lipu "Nature" (2024) pi kulupu "Google", en ilo
  "Aurorasaurus".
- Tier 2 — sitelen tan ilo toki: oko pi ilo toki li lili, la ona li ken ala
  sona e ma mun kepeken nanpa pona. taso ona li toki e ni: mun li lon, jan li
  ken lukin e ona. sitelen li jo e sona pi ma en tenpo, la kulupu li ken
  alasa e ma pi mun seli suli (fireball) en pi kule sewi (aurora). sitelen ni
  li awen lili taso; jan ante li pana e sona sama la ona li kama awen suli.


pali pi ilo
-----------

1. lipu mun: lipu pi tenpo open sina, en mun pi tenpo ni. nasin "tropical" li
   nasin open; nasin "sidereal" en nasin "house" ante li ken.
2. sewi pi tenpo pimeja ni: mun pi ken lukin, mun poka mun, sike mun (Moon
   phase), len suno (eclipse), mun tawa mute (meteor shower), mun len mun
   (occultation). ijo ale li jo e nena pi ilo sitelen.
3. lipu pi sewi (Sky Log): sona ale pi kulupu. jan mute li pana e sona sama
   la mun ni li kama "Verified Community Event". toki kulupu li lon ona.
4. mun lukin (witnessed transit): tenpo suli la lipu tenpo sina li kama lipu
   pi sewi ale pi sina.
5. lipu sona pi kulupu (Interpretation Commons): jan pi kulupu li pali e lipu
   pi sona mun; nimi ona li lon. nasin toki li ni: toki li ken taso, li lawa
   ala e sijelo en misikeke, e mani, e lawa ma.
6. kulupu jan: kulupu pi ma sama, kulupu lukin pi mun suli, en alasa kulupu
   lon ma mute pi mun len mun en mun tawa.
7. toki tawa jan: wan lon tenpo pimeja wan taso, lon tenpo ni taso: mun li
   ken lukin tan ma sina, li poka e lipu sina, kin la kon sewi li pona.


lipu ken en nasin awen
----------------------

nasin ilo (code) li AGPL-3.0. lipu en lipu sona pi kulupu li CC BY-SA 4.0.
poki sona pi mun li ODbL 1.0 anu CC0 — jan li toki ala e wan lon tenpo ni.
tenpo mute la kulupu li pana e sona ale ona tawa jan ale.

sona pona pi ma sina li lon poki len, li lon insa pi nanpa taso. jan ante li
lukin e ma suli sina taso. sina wile la sina ken pana e ma sina lon poki
open. sona pi ma lon sitelen ("EXIF") li weka lon sitelen pi jan ale. sona
tan ilo "magnetometer" en ilo "GNSS" li kama taso tan toki "ken" pi sina; ona
li weka e nimi sina lon ilo sina, li kama kulupu pi ma suli, li moku lili
taso e wawa pi ilo sina.


tenpo pali
----------

- nanpa wan: lipu mun, sewi pi tenpo pimeja ni, sitelen tan ilo toki, lipu
  tenpo pi jan wan, lipu lili pi kulupu ale.
- nanpa tu: nasin pi kama lon en kulupu sona, pana sitelen lon lipu linja
  (web) kepeken ilo "astrometry.net", lipu sona pi kulupu nanpa wan, toki
  lili, toki kulupu.
- nanpa tu wan: kulupu pi tomo sona en ilo awen, alasa kulupu, sona pi sewi
  wawa, sitelen pi ma ken lukin, pana pi poki sona, nasin open pi ilo ante.


sina ken pali e seme
--------------------

- sina jo e ilo toki: o sitelen e mun pi tenpo pimeja, o pana e sona.
- sina jo e ilo lukin mun, anu sina lon tomo sona: o pana e sitelen sina lon
  lipu linja.
- sina sona e nasin mun: o pali e lipu sona pi kulupu.
- sina sona e nasin ilo: nasin ilo li open, la sina ken pali e ona.

wile sona ale li lon lipu "OPEN-QUESTIONS.md": lipu ken pi poki sona, nasin
pi ilo "magnetometer", kulupu pi tomo sona, nasin tenpo pona tawa mun len
mun. mani li kama tan pali suli pi lipu mun, taso poki sona en lipu sona pi
kulupu li awen open tawa jan ale.
